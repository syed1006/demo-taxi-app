Some of South India's most remarkable sacred architecture sits within a half-day's drive of Bangalore — from a pillar that famously doesn't touch the ground to a Shiva linga taller than a ten-storey building. Here's the honest shortlist, ordered by distance.

## Sri Bhoga Nandeeshwara — 60 km, the time machine

At the foot of Nandi Hills stands one of Karnataka's oldest living temple complexes — begun in the **9th century** and expanded by every dynasty that followed: Ganga, Chola, Hoysala, Vijayanagara. The carved kalyani (stepped tank) is among the most photographed in the state, and weekday mornings are blissfully quiet. Pairs naturally with a [Nandi Hills sunrise](/guides/nandi-hills-sunrise-guide/).

## Adiyogi, Chikkaballapur — 65 km, the modern icon

The **112-ft Adiyogi** at Sadhguru Sannidhi isn't ancient, but it has become the region's most visited spiritual landmark. Time it for the evening **Divya Darshanam light show** if you can stay late — the [Nandi Hills + Adiyogi package (₹3,800)](/tour-packages/nandi-hills-adiyogi-1-day/) covers both with tolls and parking included.

## Kotilingeshwara, Kolar — 100 km, the surreal one

A **33-metre (108-ft) Shiva linga** — among the tallest anywhere — watched by a 35-ft Nandi and surrounded by *lakhs* of smaller lingas installed by devotees, stretching in painted rows to the horizon. Devotees can still sponsor and install a linga in their own name. The [Kotilingeshwara 1-Day Package (₹4,000)](/tour-packages/kotilingeshwara-1-day/) makes it an easy, restful day.

## Lepakshi — 120 km, the masterpiece

The 16th-century **Veerabhadra temple** is peak Vijayanagara: India's best-surviving ceiling frescoes, a monolithic Nandi the size of a bus, the giant Jatayu of Ramayana legend nearby — and the famous **hanging pillar**, which rests clear of the floor (guides will slide a cloth beneath it to prove the point). Just across the Andhra border, so carry photo ID. The [Lepakshi combo day (₹5,800)](/tour-packages/lepakshi-nandi-hills-adiyogi-1-day/) strings it with Nandi Hills and the Adiyogi.

## Tirupati — 250 km, the pilgrimage

The big one — Sri Venkateswara at Tirumala draws more pilgrims than almost any shrine on Earth, and it rewards planning over spontaneity: darshan slots, dress codes, the 22-km ghat climb. Read the [full Tirupati guide](/guides/tirupati-darshan-travel-guide/) first, then pick the [1-day dash](/tour-packages/tirupati-darshan-1-day/) or the [unhurried 2-day version (₹8,600)](/tour-packages/tirupati-2-days/).

## Don't overlook the city itself

Visiting family with only an afternoon free? The [Bangalore City Darshan (₹3,800)](/tour-packages/bangalore-city-darshan-1-day/) covers ISKCON, the Bull Temple and Tipu's mosque-adjacent old town alongside the palaces — all inside city limits.
