Kempegowda International sits a solid 30–55 km from most of Bangalore, which makes the airport transfer the one cab ride where details matter: which terminal, which exit, where the car can actually stop, and what happens when your flight is two hours late. Here's the whole picture.

## Terminals: know which one you're landing at

KIA has two terminals a few minutes apart. **Terminal 2** — the newer "garden terminal" — handles international flights and several domestic airlines; **Terminal 1** handles the rest of domestic. Airlines do move between them, so check your boarding pass or airline app rather than assuming. Your driver gets your terminal from the flight number automatically — one more reason to share it when you book.

## Where your driver actually is

After you clear baggage, follow the **Arrivals** signage out of the building. Directly outside is the **pickup lane** where private cabs wait. By the time you're out you'll already have on WhatsApp:

- the **driver's name and mobile number**,
- the **vehicle number** to spot,
- his **live location pin**.

For first-time customers the driver holds a **name board**. If the pickup lane is chaotic (peak-hour international arrivals), he'll direct you to the exact pillar number to stand at — a 30-second walk beats a 15-minute crawl through the lane.

## The parts nobody tells you

- **The toll:** the airport expressway toll is billed at actuals on top of the fare — it's a small, receipted amount.
- **Airport parking:** if you ask the driver to come into the multi-level car park and help with luggage (families, elders), the parking charge is at actuals. The pickup lane itself avoids this.
- **Departures are simpler:** for drops, the car pulls into the departures ramp right outside your terminal — no parking involved.

## What it costs

Sedan transfers **start at ₹899**, which covers pickups within roughly 40 km of the airport — Hebbal, Malleshwaram, MG Road, Koramangala, Indiranagar and most of the city core. Farther corridors (Whitefield ₹899, Electronic City ₹1,099) have their own fixed fares — the full area-by-area list is on the [Bangalore airport taxi page](/airport-taxi-bangalore/). Fixed means fixed: no surge at 2 AM, no "airport charges" invented at the kerb.
