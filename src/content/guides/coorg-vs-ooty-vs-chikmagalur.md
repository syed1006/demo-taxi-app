Three hill escapes, three completely different moods — and everyone in Bangalore eventually argues about which one wins. Having driven a few hundred families to all three, here's our drivers' honest scorecard.

## The quick comparison

| | Coorg | Ooty | Chikmagalur |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Distance | ~250 km | ~280 km | ~245 km |
| Drive time | 5–6 hrs | 7–8 hrs | 5–6 hrs |
| Altitude vibe | Coffee valleys | Proper mountain town | Coffee + peaks |
| Signature sights | Monastery, Dubare, Abbey Falls | Botanical Gardens, toy train, Doddabetta | Mullayanagiri, Hebbe Falls, estates |
| Crowds | High on weekends | Highest (holiday season) | Lowest |
| Ideal length | 2 days | 3 days (with Mysore) | 2 days |
| Special rule | Mandalpatti needs a local jeep | Bandipur gate shuts 9 PM–6 AM | Ghat roads after Belur |

## Coorg — the all-rounder

Nowhere else packs this much variety per kilometre: Tibetan gold at Bylakuppe, elephants at Dubare, the Raja's Seat sunset, a 4x4 scramble to Mandalpatti and the Kaveri's source at Talakaveri. Food is its own attraction — pandi curry and estate coffee. Weekends get busy, but the [field-tested 2-day itinerary](/guides/coorg-2-day-itinerary/) routes around the worst of it. **Pick Coorg if:** it's your first hill trip, you have exactly one weekend, or the group has mixed ages. ## Ooty — the classic

The only true mountain town of the three — a colonial hill station at 2,240 m with a lake, a steam toy train and the Nilgiris rolling to the horizon. It's also the longest drive, gated by [Bandipur's night closure](/guides/bangalore-to-ooty-road-guide/), which is why we pair it with Mysore in a [3-day loop](/tour-packages/ooty-mysore-3-days/) rather than sell a rushed weekend. **Pick Ooty if:** you want cool-air Britain-in-the-hills atmosphere, you're combining with Mysore, or the toy train is on someone's bucket list.

## Chikmagalur — the quiet one

Karnataka's coffee heartland without the tour-bus circuit: Mullayanagiri (the state's highest peak) for sunrise, Baba Budangiri's ridgeline, Hebbe Falls deep inside an estate, and homestays where the silence is the point. **Pick Chikmagalur if:** you're a couple or small group chasing calm, you've already done Coorg, or you want weekday-empty viewpoints.
