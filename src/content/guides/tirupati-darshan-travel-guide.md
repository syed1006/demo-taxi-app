Tirupati is the most-planned pilgrimage out of Bangalore, and the plan really matters: the difference between a serene darshan and a twelve-hour queue is mostly decided before you leave home. Here's what to sort out, in order.

## First: book the darshan, then the travel

The temple at **Tirumala** (the hill above Tirupati town) offers several darshan tracks:

- **Sarva Darshan** — free, no booking, and the queue can run many hours on busy days.
- **Special Entry Darshan (₹300)** — timed slots booked on the official **TTD portal**, typically a 1–3 hour experience. This is the one to get.
- **Senior citizen / divyangula darshan** — separate provisions exist; check the TTD site for current rules.

Weekend and festival slots sell out well in advance. Book the slot first, then plan the cab around it — not the other way round.

## One day or two?

**The one-day run** (about 250 km each way) works with a 4–5 AM start: NH-75 out through Chittoor district, up the ghat by late morning, darshan in your slot, laddu counter, and back in Bangalore late night. It's a long, satisfying day — doable with a confirmed slot, and we'll quote it as a [round-trip taxi](/bangalore-to-tirupati-taxi/) or a custom day plan on WhatsApp.

**The two-day version** is kinder: reach by lunch, evening at **Sri Padmavathi Ammavari Temple** in Tiruchanur (customarily visited alongside the main darshan), overnight in Tirupati town, morning darshan slot, unhurried return. That's the [2-day package at ₹8,600](/tour-packages/tirupati-2-days/) — note that tolls, parking and the **Andhra Pradesh state permit** are at actuals on this route.

## The details that trip people up

- **The ghat road:** 22 km of hairpins from town to temple. Prone-to-motion-sickness travellers should sit front, and yes — AC off on the steepest stretch, as on all ghats.
- **Dress code:** traditional wear is mandatory for Special Entry. Jeans get turned away.
- **Phones/cameras:** not allowed inside — leave them with the driver.
- **The laddu:** collect at the counters after darshan with your darshan receipt; carry small cash.
- **Hair tonsure:** the Kalyanakatta halls handle tonsure offerings; go early if this is part of your vow.
