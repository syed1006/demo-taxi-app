Coorg rewards a plan. Its sights are scattered across 60 km of winding coffee country, and the difference between a relaxed weekend and a day spent staring at hairpin bends is simply the order you do things in. This is the routing our drivers have settled on after hundreds of runs — the same one built into our [Coorg 2-Day Package](/tour-packages/coorg-2-days/).

## Day 1 — the Kushalnagar belt on the way in

Leave Bangalore at **5:30–6:00 AM**. The five-hour run goes via Mysore Road, with breakfast en route. The genius of day one is that Coorg's eastern sights line up right on your approach road:

- **Golden Temple (Namdroling Monastery), Bylakuppe** — the great Tibetan settlement's gilded prayer halls; arrive around 11 AM before tour-bus rush.
- **Kaveri Nisargadhama** — a bamboo river island with deer and a hanging bridge; an easy post-lunch hour.
- **Dubare Elephant Camp** — cross the river by boat; elephant interactions wind down by late afternoon, so don't leave it past 4.
- **Raja's Seat, Madikeri** — arrive by 5:45 PM for the sunset over the valleys, then check in.

## Day 2 — go high, then go holy

Start early: **Mandalpatti viewpoint** at 7 AM means mist below you rather than haze around you. Your cab drops you at the jeep point; local 4x4s (paid there) do the rough final stretch. Back down for **Abbey Falls** by 10 — a waterfall inside a coffee estate, thunderous after the rains — then **Omkareshwara Temple** in Madikeri town.

After lunch, drive west to **Bhagamandala**, where three rivers meet at the Triveni Sangama, and climb to **Talakaveri**, the source of the Kaveri on Brahmagiri hill. Leave by 4 PM and you're home in Bangalore around 10.

## The practical bits

- **AC off on the ghats** — standard practice on steep climbs, not a cab defect.
- **Carry cash** for the Mandalpatti jeep, entry tickets and estate coffee purchases.
- **Monsoon plan B**: when rain closes Mandalpatti, swap in the Cauvery-side sights or a coffee-estate tour — decide with your driver in the morning.
- **Food to hunt down**: pandi curry (Coorg pork), akki roti, and bamboo-shoot curry in season.
