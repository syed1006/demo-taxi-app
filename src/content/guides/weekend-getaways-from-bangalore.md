Bangalore's real luxury isn't in the city — it's what sits within three hundred kilometres of it. Here are the seven weekend escapes we drive most, ordered by distance, with the honest version of what each is like.

## 1. Nandi Hills — 60 km, the half-day classic

Sunrise above the clouds, an old hill fortress, breakfast, home by noon. The formula works — read the [full sunrise guide](/guides/nandi-hills-sunrise-guide/) for timings, or see [fares](/bangalore-to-nandi-hills-taxi/).

## 2. Lepakshi — 120 km, the art-history day out

A 16th-century Vijayanagara temple just across the Andhra border with India's most famous **hanging pillar**, giant frescoes and a monolithic Nandi. Combine it with Nandi Hills and the Adiyogi in [one ambitious day](/tour-packages/lepakshi-nandi-hills-adiyogi-1-day/). [Route details →](/bangalore-to-lepakshi-taxi/)

## 3. Hogenakkal Falls — 130 km, the raw one

The Kaveri crashes through a granite gorge on the Tamil Nadu border; coracle rides drift you right to the spray. Best July–October when the river is full. [Route details →](/bangalore-to-hogenakkal-taxi/)

## 4. Shivanasamudra — 135 km, twin waterfalls

Gaganachukki and Bharachukki — two huge falls on one river island, at their thundering best after the monsoon. Pairs beautifully with Somanathapura's star-shaped Hoysala temple. [Route details →](/bangalore-to-shivanasamudra-taxi/)

## 5. Mysore — 145 km, the complete package

Palace, Chamundi Hills, Srirangapatna, the zoo, Brindavan's fountains — the deepest single destination on this list, now 90 minutes away by expressway. Do it as a [packed one-day tour](/tour-packages/mysore-1-day-tour/) or read the [road guide](/guides/bangalore-to-mysore-road-guide/) and go at your own pace.

## 6. Chikmagalur — 245 km, coffee and peaks

Coffee estates, Mullayanagiri (Karnataka's highest peak) and waterfall trails. This one wants an overnight — the [2-day package](/tour-packages/chikmagalur-2-days/) covers the car for ₹8,600. [Route details →](/bangalore-to-chikmagalur-taxi/)

## 7. Coorg — 250 km, the crowd favourite

Monasteries, elephants, viewpoints and the source of the Kaveri. Two days minimum; the [field-tested itinerary](/guides/coorg-2-day-itinerary/) shows exactly how to route it.

**Booking any of these:** every route page lists fixed one-way and round-trip fares, or take a [package](/tour-packages/) and let the itinerary come pre-planned.
