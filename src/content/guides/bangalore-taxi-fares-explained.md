Every taxi site says "affordable" — few print the actual numbers. Here are ours, the same rates our drivers quote, organised by how you'll use the cab. All fares include fuel; tolls, parking and permits are at actuals.

## Airport transfers (fixed, both directions)

| Vehicle | Base fare (≤40 km) |
| --- | --- |
| Sedan (4+1) | ₹899 |
| SUV — Innova/Ertiga (6–7+1) | ₹1,499 |
| Innova Crysta (6+1) | ₹1,999 |
| Tempo Traveller (12+1) | ₹3,200 |

The base covers pickups within about 40 km of Kempegowda Airport — most of the city. Farther corridors (Electronic City, Sarjapur) are a little more; the [area-wise list](/airport-taxi-bangalore/) has every exact fare. Flight tracking and 24/7 service included; no night charges.

## Outstation — round trips

| Vehicle | Rate | Driver bata/day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sedan | ₹13/km | ₹400 |
| SUV | ₹18/km | ₹500 |
| Innova Crysta | ₹21/km | ₹600 |
| Tempo Traveller | ₹24/km | ₹600 |

Billing is on the full round-trip distance with a **300 km/day minimum**. Popular corridors skip the math entirely with fixed fares — Mysore ₹5,800 round trip, Coorg, Ooty, Tirupati and 17 more on the [routes page](/outstation-cabs-bangalore/).

## Outstation — one-way drops

Sedan ₹18/km, SUV ₹24/km, Crysta ₹30/km, Tempo ₹34/km — one side only, since the car returns empty. Twenty corridors have fixed drop fares from ₹2,100; see the [one-way page](/one-way-cab-bangalore/) and the [one-way vs round-trip guide](/guides/one-way-vs-round-trip-cab/) for which model saves you money.

## Tour packages: one price, everything planned

For sightseeing, the [tour packages](/tour-packages/) usually beat raw per-km math: Mysore's 8-place day is ₹5,800 all-inclusive (tolls and parking too), Coorg's 2-day loop is ₹8,600, and the 3-day trips run ₹12,900 — each with the itinerary already built.

## What's never on our bill

Surge pricing, night multipliers, "AC extra", cancellation fees or advance payments. You pay after the trip — cash, UPI or card.
