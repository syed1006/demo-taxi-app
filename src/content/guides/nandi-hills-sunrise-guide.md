Nandi Hills at sunrise is Bangalore's favourite early-morning ritual for a reason: forty hairpin bends up an ancient hill fortress, and — on a good morning — a carpet of clouds below your feet with the sun burning through it. Done right it's magical; done wrong it's a parking queue in the dark. Here's the done-right version.

## The timing math

Everything hinges on the **6 AM gate opening**. Sunrise runs roughly 5:50–6:20 AM depending on season, and the best light — the soft pre-sun glow over the cloud line — comes in the first half hour. From central Bangalore it's about 60 km / **90 minutes** on empty pre-dawn roads (NH-44 past the airport turn-off, then the hill road from Devanahalli side).

So: **pickup at 4:15–4:30 AM**, gates by 6:00, Tipu's Drop by 6:15. On winter weekends, cars start queuing before the gates open and the hilltop parking fills by 7:30 — a weekday visit is a different, calmer experience entirely.

## What it costs at the gate

Entry is a small per-person fee (around ₹30) plus vehicle parking, paid at the gate in cash. On busy weekends the authorities sometimes cap vehicle entry once parking fills — one more reason the early start matters.

## What to carry

- **A warm layer.** The summit sits at 1,478 m and runs several degrees colder than the city, with real wind at Tipu's Drop.
- **Cash** for the gate, chai and breakfast stalls.
- **Nothing loose in your hands** — the monkeys near the viewpoints are professionals.
