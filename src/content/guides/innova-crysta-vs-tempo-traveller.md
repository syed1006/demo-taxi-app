Group trips die by vehicle choice: squeeze nine people into seven seats and the Western Ghats will make everyone regret it. Here's the honest comparison between the two workhorses of Karnataka group travel.

## The 30-second answer

- **6–7 travellers →** [Innova Crysta](/innova-crysta-rental-bangalore/). Premium captain seats, car-like ride, ₹21/km outstation.
- **8–12 travellers →** [Tempo Traveller](/tempo-traveller-hire-bangalore/). Everyone in one vehicle, pushback seats, ₹24/km outstation.
- **13+ travellers →** 17-seater Tempo on request, or two vehicles.

The trap to avoid is the middle: eight people "adjusting" in a Crysta works for an airport run and ruins a five-hour highway day. Count adults honestly, then add the luggage.

## Comfort: different kinds of good

The **Crysta** is the premium experience — soft captain seats in the middle row, quiet cabin, rear AC vents, and highway stability that makes 300 km days feel short. It's what we send for client-facing travel and comfort-first family holidays.

The **Tempo Traveller** wins on *space per person*: proper aisle to stretch into, pushback seats, and room for the party to actually feel like a party. On overnight starts, people genuinely sleep. Music system and ducted AC are standard on our units.

## Luggage: the overlooked deal-breaker

Seven passengers in a Crysta means the boot is nearly full before bags. For airport-heavy trips or wedding groups with serious luggage, the Tempo's rear luggage bay swallows what a Crysta cannot. Rule: if every traveller has a big suitcase, size up.

## The money math

For a two-day, 600-km Coorg-style weekend: a Crysta at ₹21/km runs about ₹13,800 including bata; a Tempo at ₹24/km about ₹15,600. Split across 6 people, the Crysta costs ~₹2,300 a head; split across 11, the Tempo costs ~₹1,400 a head. **The bigger the group, the harder the Tempo wins.** For popular routes you can skip the math entirely — [tour packages](/tour-packages/) carry fixed prices for both classes wherever we offer them.
